Ahead

Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026

Ahead is a personal Android calendar and task planner. It has no Ahead account, advertising, analytics service, or hosted Ahead database.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google is optional. With your permission, Ahead reads your list of subscribed calendars and reads, creates, edits, and deletes calendar events when you use those features. Event information can include titles, times, notes, locations, and repeat patterns. Ahead communicates directly with Google’s Calendar API to provide calendar display, search, and editing.

Google account authorization is managed by Google Play services. Ahead keeps short-lived access tokens in app memory rather than its calendar cache. It does not request your Google password. Google’s services are governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

Data on your device

Ahead stores downloaded calendar information, your Google account identifier, pending calendar changes, tasks, focus history, and preferences in its private app storage. Pending changes are sent to Google when a connection is available and a sync runs. Device or operating-system backups may include app data according to your phone settings.

Phone calendar access is optional and separate from Google authorization. Ahead uses it to display and manage calendars available through Android. Task reminders use Android’s local notification system.

Weather and schedule notifications

Weather is optional. Ahead sends the coordinates of your chosen city to Open-Meteo to request a forecast, and city search terms to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service. These requests also expose the network IP address to that service. Ahead does not request GPS access or track your movements. Calendar titles, notes, locations, and account identifiers are not sent to the weather service. You can change the city or turn off weather in Settings. Forecasts are cached on your device.

With notification permission, Ahead can schedule a morning briefing and event alerts on your device. These use your downloaded schedule, tasks, and forecast, and can include event titles, times, and locations. Notification previews on your lock screen are controlled through your phone’s notification settings. The notifications do not use an AI service or a notification server.

Sharing and retention

Ahead does not sell your data, use it for advertising, send it to an Ahead server, or use it to train AI models. Calendar changes are sent to the calendar provider you choose. If another AI service creates events in Google Calendar, that service operates separately from Ahead.

Disconnecting Google in Ahead revokes Ahead’s Google authorization and removes its downloaded Google calendar cache. Send or discard pending changes first. Disconnecting does not delete events from Google Calendar. Clearing Ahead’s app data or uninstalling the app removes its local tasks and preferences; separately retained operating-system backups are controlled through your phone settings. You can also revoke access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

Google user data

Ahead’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on Ahead’s Google consent screen.
